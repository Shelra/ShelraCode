## A. Papers — review bottleneck and productivity

1. **The Productivity-Reliability Paradox: Specification-Driven Governance for AI-Augmented Software Development** — Sabry E. Farrag — submitted 1 May 2026 — arXiv:2605.01160 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01160 — Source of the "+98% PRs / +91% review time / flat delivery / 19% RCT slowdown / 20–56% gains" compilation. Single-author position paper; **the underlying telemetry study is not identified and I could not find it — the "91%" provenance is UNVERIFIED.**
2. **AI Writes Faster Than Humans Can Review: A Longitudinal Study of an Enterprise 2x Mandate** — He, Agarwal, Denisov-Blanch, Azaletskiy, Koyejo, Vasilescu — 2 Jul 2026 — arXiv:2607.01904 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01904 (HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2607.01904v1) — The lane's single most important source. 802 devs, 196,212 PRs, Jan 2024–Apr 2026: human review coverage 89%→68%, automated review ~19%→~84%, per-reviewer load 2.0×, PR volume 3.1×, reviewer pool 1.5×, output 2.09×, AI PRs +20% review-to-merge and +22% cycle time, merge rate flat, revert rate slightly down.
3. **Automating Low-Risk Code Review at Meta: RADAR, Risk Calibration, and Review Efficiency** — Meta (30 authors incl. Mockus, Rigby, Nagappan) — 28 May 2026, rev. 12 Jun 2026 — arXiv:2605.30208 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30208 — 535K+ diffs, 331K+ landed, 60.31% auto-approval at the 50th-percentile threshold, revert rate 1/3 and incident rate 1/50 of non-RADAR, median review wall time −35%.
4. **Adoption and Impact of Command-Line AI Coding Agents: A Study of Microsoft's Early 2026 Rollout of Claude Code and GitHub Copilot CLI** — Murphy-Hill, Butler, Savelieva (Microsoft) — 1 Jul 2026 — arXiv:2607.01418 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01418 — +24% merged PRs among adopters (range 14.5–33.7% per secondary reporting), >50% for 5+-day users vs ~15% for 3-day; observational, not an RCT; "a merged PR is not the same as the value it delivers".
5. **3100 Opinions on Code Review in an AI World: Building Causal Theory from Practitioner Discourse** — Agarwal, Miller, Kästner, Vasilescu — 8 Jul 2026 — arXiv:2607.07980 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07980 (HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2607.07980v1) — 38,709 grey-lit docs, 3,100 coded; 26 constructs, 67 relationships (3 contested); the "linear vs multiplicative" quote (G2802) and the 200-PR skimming quote (G2048).
6. **Trust-Calibrated Code Review: A Participatory Design Study of Review Workflows for LLM-Generated Multi-File Changes** — Heander, Sergeyuk, Zakharov, Söderberg, Mukhortov — 1 Jun 2026 — arXiv:2606.01969 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01969 — 17 practitioners, 7 returning, 43 validating; three-level review architecture; ~63% anticipated reduced review effort.
